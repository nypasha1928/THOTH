      LIVE strategy & risk

        SMA 7/50 exits use the forming candle and close only, for all entry strategies. Checked approximately every 5 seconds while connected; these exits are server-managed. Enabling applies to existing positions too. The S/R 1h → Exit 1m strategy always uses 1-minute exits and sampled hourly entries; other strategies use their selected timeframe. Pause entries before changing settings. Existing positions keep their original dollar stop.

        Daily protection uses starting equity × your percentage as a fixed dollar allowance below the day’s highest transfer-adjusted equity. It closes every managed position, including winners. Server restarts require manually enabling entries; a missed midnight baseline requires a flat-account review. Per-coin loss-streak pauses block only that coin’s new entries; stops and opposite-signal exits stay managed.

        The optional overall stop counts losing P&L only; winning gains never offset it. Choose whether its denominator uses losing positions’ initial margin or all open positions’ initial margin. It closes every managed position and requires manual restart.
