# Order Block Strength: intrabar versus hourly close

Analysis contains a collapsed **Order Block Strength · Intrabar versus 1h Close** panel. Observation is ON by default. No strategy selection or LIVE activation is changed. The market worker must remain connected with `crypto_ob_strength_1h_close` selected. Collection continues with the panel collapsed and entry permission paused. Existing hourly downloads are reused; there are no extra requests to Bybit.

The observer owns a separate SQLite database and bounded background queue. Closed hourly data advance its private native strength checkpoint; each forming-hour preview runs on a copy. Only formations are used, with the existing per-symbol raw-price width thresholds. A first/startup/reconnection sample establishes a baseline rather than replaying an old signal. Each fresh direction is consumed at most once per symbol/hour. First early evidence, subsequent direction changes, latest preview, boundaries/strength, hourly-close outcome and signed early-price advantage are retained. Positive advantage means the first early observation had a better price than the confirmed candle close in the same direction. This does not compare exchange fills.

Two independent paper legs per coin compare early and observed closed-hour entries. Both use the saved notional, hard stop and per-coin trailing settings, with 5.5bps fees and 2bps adverse slippage per side. Opposite qualifying formations close/reverse. Prices, stops and peaks use received snapshots, not invented intrabar highs/lows. A $5 activation with $0.10 drop therefore requires a sampled gross $5 profit. Settings changes form separate identities. Settings and paper positions persist across restarts. Gaps exceeding 120 seconds, disabling observation, or revised confirmed candles invalidate open paper episodes; they are counted as unverified, not closed for fabricated profit. Old missed closes do not become retrospective entries.

Paper net totals exclude open P&L and cover retained identities. These independent coin episodes **do not** implement portfolio capacity, combined trailing, account stops, trading schedules, funding, liquidation, exchange quantity constraints or real settlement. They are a sampled timing comparison, not a prediction of live account returns. Missing between-scan prices can miss peaks/stops/signals. Keep the live closed-hour results separate.

Refresh, pagination, per-row expandable evidence and **Copy all comparisons** are available. Export contains all retained rows (up to 5,000); settings states are bounded to 300 symbol/identity records. Observer errors/dropped samples are visible and cannot alter LIVE decisions. `/api/live/crypto-ob-comparison`, `/export`, and `/settings` expose the isolated observer only.
